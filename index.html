JH-ARC

Jeju Haenyeo Aging and Resilience Cohort

What does a lifetime of breath-hold diving and cold-water exposure leave behind in ageing and disease?

5 m

The cohort

Haenyeo are women who have dived for shellfish off Jeju without breathing apparatus, often for fifty years or more. We follow them against matched island residents using hospital records linked to deep phenotyping.

Participants
8,868 haenyeo · 26,604 matched controls (1:3)
Observation
2004–2023 · Jeju National University Hospital CDW
Linkage
Jeju Genome Project · deep phenotyping, multi-omics
Design
Matched retrospective cohort · clinical text from 2009
